B. Bài tập ngữ pháp

Mức 1 — Nhận biết (Chọn/điền tính từ sở hữu đúng)

  1. This is Nam. ______ (He / His) favourite subject is Math.
  2. I love ______ (my / me) new school.
  3. Lan and Mai are friends. ______ (Their / They) classroom is on the second floor.
  4. We have ______ (our / us) English lesson on Monday.
  5. The school has ______ (its / it) own library.

Mức 2 — Vận dụng (Hoàn thành câu theo ngữ cảnh)

  1. Tom has a new bag. ______ bag is blue. (Điền tính từ sở hữu phù hợp với "Tom")
  2. I have Geography today. ______ Geography book is in ______ locker. (Điền 2 tính từ sở hữu, ngôi thứ nhất số ít)
  3. Viết lại câu sau dùng tính từ sở hữu: "Mai and Nam have History on Tuesday." → ______ History class is on Tuesday.
  4. You and I share a timetable. ______ timetable has six subjects.
  5. The teacher has a new classroom. ______ classroom is next to the gym.

Mức 3 — Nâng cao

  1. Viết lại câu, thay các danh từ sở hữu gạch chân bằng tính từ sở hữu đúng: "Lan's school has a big playground. Lan's classmates love Lan's school."
  2. Điền đúng 3 tính từ sở hữu khác nhau vào đoạn sau: "Hi, I'm Phong. This is ______ new school. ______ classroom is very nice, and ______ classmates are friendly."
  3. Viết 3-4 câu ngắn giới thiệu về môn học yêu thích của em và bạn em, dùng ít nhất 2 tính từ sở hữu khác nhau (ví dụ: my, his/her).

C. Đọc hiểu

Hi, my name is Phong. I am a new student at Green School. My first day was exciting but I was also a little nervous. My classroom is on the first floor, next to the library. My favourite subjects are Math and Art. Our timetable has six subjects every day, and PE is on Wednesday. I keep my books in my locker near the gym. My new friend Minh sits next to me. His favourite subject is Science. We are happy at our new school.
  1. What is Phong's favourite subject? A. Music B. Math and Art C. History
  2. Where is Phong's classroom?
  3. Which day does Phong have PE?
  4. Where does Phong keep his books?
  5. What is Minh's favourite subject?

B. Bài tập ngữ pháp

Mức 1 — Nhận biết (Chọn There is / There are đúng)

  1. ______ a big playground in my school. (There is / There are)
  2. ______ forty students in my class. (There is / There are)
  3. ______ a canteen next to the gym. (There is / There are)
  4. We have English ______ Monday. (in / on / at)
  5. School starts ______ 7 o'clock. (in / on / at)

Mức 2 — Vận dụng (Hoàn thành câu theo ngữ cảnh)

  1. Viết lại câu dùng "There is": "My school / a library / on the second floor." → ______
  2. Viết lại câu dùng "There are": "Six subjects / in our timetable." → ______
  3. Điền giới từ đúng: We have PE ______ the morning and Art ______ the afternoon.
  4. Điền giới từ đúng: My class has History ______ Tuesday ______ 9 o'clock.
  5. Viết câu phủ định với "There isn't/aren't": "a gym / in the old building" (không có phòng thể chất trong tòa nhà cũ). → ______

Mức 3 — Nâng cao

  1. Viết lại thành câu hỏi và trả lời ngắn: "There are two canteens in the school." → Câu hỏi: ______? Trả lời ngắn: ______.
  2. Điền đúng cả "there is/there are" và giới từ thời gian còn thiếu: "______ a new timetable this year. We have Music ______ Friday ______ the afternoon."
  3. Viết 3-4 câu ngắn miêu tả trường của em, dùng ít nhất 2 câu "There is/There are" và ít nhất 1 giới từ chỉ thời gian (in/on/at).

C. Đọc hiểu

Welcome to Sunrise School! There is a large playground behind the school, and there are two canteens for students. There is a library on the third floor. We have classes in the morning and clubs in the afternoon. On Monday, we have a flag ceremony at 7 o'clock. There are twelve classrooms and one gym. Some new students feel nervous on the first day, but they soon feel excited about their new school.
  1. Is there a playground at Sunrise School? A. Yes, there is. B. No, there isn't.
  2. How many canteens are there?
  3. Where is the library?
  4. What time is the flag ceremony on Monday?
  5. How do new students feel at first, and how do they feel later?

  1. My sister has a new bag. ______ (Her / She) bag is pink.
  2. ______ a big gym in our school. (There is / There are)
  3. We have Music ______ Friday. (in / on / at)
  4. ______ two libraries in this school. (There is / There are)
  5. Class starts ______ 7:30. (in / on / at)

Mức 2 — Vận dụng

  1. Viết lại câu: "Nam and Minh / a Science lesson / on Thursday / at 8 o'clock" (dùng tính từ sở hữu). → ______
  2. Viết lại câu dùng "There is/There are": "My school / one canteen / three libraries." → ______ / ______
  3. Điền tính từ sở hữu và giới từ còn thiếu: "This is ______ (I) timetable. We have Art ______ Wednesday ______ the morning."
  4. Viết câu hỏi cho câu trả lời: "Yes, there are two canteens." → ______?
  5. Điền đúng: "Lan feels ______ (nervous) on the first day, but she feels ______ (excited) about ______ (she) new friends."

Mức 3 — Nâng cao

  1. Viết lại đoạn sau, sửa 3 lỗi sai (tính từ sở hữu, there is/are, giới từ thời gian): "There is two canteens in my school. Our locker are near the gym. We have English in Monday at 7 o'clock."
  2. Điền đúng cả tính từ sở hữu, there is/are và giới từ vào đoạn: "Hi, I'm Mai. This is ______ new school. ______ a big playground here. We have PE ______ Tuesday ______ the afternoon, and ______ classmates are very friendly."
  3. Viết một đoạn văn ngắn 4-5 câu giới thiệu trường mới của em, dùng ít nhất: 1 tính từ sở hữu, 1 câu "There is/There are", 1 giới từ chỉ thời gian (in/on/at).

C. Đọc hiểu

Last Monday was Hoa's first day at Blue Sky School. She felt nervous in the morning, but she felt excited by the afternoon. There are twenty classrooms and one big library in her school. Her classroom has a colourful timetable on the wall. Hoa has Math and English in the morning, and Art in the afternoon. Her new friend, Lan, sits next to her. Their favourite subject is Science, and they have it on Thursday at 9 o'clock. Hoa keeps her books in her locker near the canteen.
  1. How did Hoa feel in the morning, and how did she feel in the afternoon?
  2. How many classrooms are there at Blue Sky School? A. Ten B. Twenty C. Thirty
  3. What subjects does Hoa have in the morning?
  4. What is Hoa and Lan's favourite subject, and when do they have it?
  5. Where does Hoa keep her books?

My new house

My family has just moved to a new house in the countryside. It is not very big, but it is very cosy. There is a living room, a kitchen and a dining room downstairs. I really love our new house.

  1. Where has the family just moved to?
  2. Is the house big?
  3. How many bedrooms are there upstairs?
  4. Is there a garage?
  5. What does the writer's mother grow in the garden?

Đáp án Chuyên đề 1 — Tính từ sở hữu

Mức 1: 1. Her (chỉ Mai, giống cái) — 2. Our (chủ ngữ "We") — 3. My (chủ ngữ "I") — 4. Its (chỉ con vật) — 5. Their (chủ ngữ "Nam and Lan" = they)
Mức 2: 1. My — 2. Your — 3. His (Mr. Ha là nam) — 4. Our (my family = chúng tôi) — 5. Their (those birds = chúng nó)
Mức 3:
1. This is his pen. (Tom là nam, "the pen belongs to Tom" → pen của anh ấy = his pen)
2. Sửa: "Lan and I love our school." (Lan và I = chúng tôi, không dùng "my")
3. (1) His — (2) His — (3) his (đều chỉ Ba, giống đực)
4. This is our picture.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 2 — There is/There are

Mức 1: 1. is (a lamp - số ít) — 2. are (many trees - số nhiều) — 3. Is (a bathroom - số ít) — 4. aren't (any chairs - số nhiều, phủ định) — 5. is (water - danh từ không đếm được)
Mức 2:
1. There is a fridge in the kitchen.
2. There are three pictures on the wall.
3. There aren't any dogs in the garden.
4. Is there a computer in your room?
5. There are some flowers in the vase.
Mức 3:
1. There are two bedrooms in my house. (kết hợp tính từ sở hữu "my")
2. Sửa: "There are many books on his shelf." (many books - số nhiều nên dùng are)
3. Is there a pen in her bag? - Yes, there is.
4. There are thirty-five students in our class.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 3 — Giới từ nơi chốn & thời gian

Mức 1: 1. on (bề mặt bàn) — 2. behind (phía sau nhà) — 3. at (giờ cụ thể 6 a.m.) — 4. on (thứ Hai) — 5. in (mùa đông)
Mức 2:
1. The cat is between the two dogs.
2. School starts at 7:00.
3. Tet holiday is in January or February.
4. The bookshop is next to the bakery.
5. I do my homework in the evening.
Mức 3:
1. There is a lamp between the bed and the desk.
2. Sửa: "My class begins at 8 o'clock." (giờ cụ thể dùng "at", đồng thời "class" là ngôi thứ 3 số ít nên "begin" phải chia thành "begins")
3. (1) in — (2) In — (3) in
4. There is a bookshop between my house and the school.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 4 — Thì hiện tại đơn

Mức 1: 1. goes (she - ngôi 3 số ít) — 2. work (my parents - số nhiều) — 3. doesn't (he - ngôi 3 số ít) — 4. Do (you) — 5. studies (my sister - ngôi 3 số ít, "study" tận cùng phụ âm+y → đổi y thành i, thêm es)
Mức 2:
1. He watches TV every night. (watch tận cùng -ch → thêm es)
2. They don't go to school on Sunday.
3. Does your mother cook dinner?
4. Lan finishes her homework before 9 p.m. (finish tận cùng -sh → thêm es)
5. I don't like spicy food.
Mức 3:
1. My father goes to work at 7 a.m. every day. (kết hợp giới từ "at" chỉ giờ)
2. Sửa: "My brother doesn't like vegetables." (đã có "doesn't" thì động từ theo sau phải ở dạng nguyên thể, bỏ "s")
3. gets / at / in (get up → chia theo "my mother", giới từ "at 6 o'clock", "in the morning")
4. My brother doesn't play chess on Sunday.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 5 — So sánh hơn & so sánh nhất

Mức 1: 1. cheaper (so sánh hơn với "than") — 2. more difficult (difficult là tính từ dài) — 3. tallest (đi với "the", so sánh nhất) — 4. more beautiful (beautiful là tính từ dài) — 5. hotter (hot là tính từ ngắn, tận cùng phụ âm-nguyên âm-phụ âm nên gấp đôi "t")
Mức 2:
1. Nam is younger than Ba. (12 tuổi ít hơn 13 tuổi)
2. This is the most difficult exercise.
3. The red shirt is more expensive than the blue shirt.
4. Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
5. Her handwriting is better than his. (good - better - best là bất quy tắc)
Mức 3:
1. My father is the most careful driver in my family.
2. Sửa: "This film is more interesting than that film." (interesting là tính từ dài, phải dùng "more" chứ không thêm "than" trực tiếp sau "interesting")
3. studies / harder / the best (chia đúng ngôi thứ 3 số ít "Mai studies"; "hard" là tính từ ngắn nên +er; "good" là bất quy tắc → the best)
4. The elephant is bigger than the dog, but the dog is smarter than the elephant.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 6 — Should/shouldn't và Ought to

Mức 1: 1. should (lời khuyên nên ăn nhiều rau) — 2. shouldn't (không nên ngủ muộn) — 3. Should (câu hỏi xin lời khuyên) — 4. ought to (đúng cấu trúc, không có "s") — 5. shouldn't (không nên xả rác)
Mức 2:
1. You should study hard for the exam.
2. You shouldn't play games too much.
3. You should see a doctor.
4. Students shouldn't talk in class.
5. We ought to wear a helmet when riding a motorbike.
Mức 3:
1. Nam should go to bed earlier. / Nam shouldn't go to bed so late. (dựa trên tình huống anh ấy mệt mỏi mỗi sáng)
2. Sửa: "You should eat breakfast every morning." (should + V nguyên thể, không có "to")
3. You should choose English because it is the most useful subject. (kết hợp so sánh nhất)
4. We shouldn't talk in the library.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 7 — Must/mustn't

Mức 1: 1. must (bắt buộc an toàn) — 2. mustn't (cấm đi trễ) — 3. mustn't (cấm xả rác) — 4. must (bắt buộc hoàn thành bài tập) — 5. mustn't (cấm hút thuốc)
Mức 2:
1. Students must wear the uniform.
2. Students mustn't run in the corridor.
3. We must keep quiet in the library.
4. You mustn't cross the street when the light is red.
5. We must turn off the lights before going out.
Mức 3:
1. You should look both ways before crossing the street. (lời khuyên) / You must wear a helmet when riding a motorbike. (bắt buộc)
2. Sửa: "You must keep silent in the exam room." (must + V nguyên thể, không có "to")
3. (1) must — (2) mustn't — (3) should (làm bài tập là bắt buộc; nói chuyện riêng là bị cấm; giúp bạn là lời khuyên nên làm, không bắt buộc)
4. You mustn't eat in the classroom, but you should bring a bottle of water.

Đáp án Chuyên đề 8 — Would like to & Hiện tại tiếp diễn tương lai

Mức 1: 1. would like (diễn tả mong muốn lịch sự) — 2. Would (câu hỏi lịch sự) — 3. is meeting (kế hoạch đã sắp xếp cho "tomorrow afternoon") — 4. are going (kế hoạch cho "next Sunday") — 5. wouldn't like (không muốn, diễn đạt lịch sự)
Mức 2:
1. I would like to visit Ha Long Bay this summer.
2. We are playing badminton tomorrow morning.
3. What would you like to have for dinner?
4. We aren't going to school next Monday.
5. I would like to become a doctor in the future.
Mức 3:
1. Gợi ý: A: What would you like to do this weekend? B: I would like to visit my grandparents. I am leaving on Saturday morning. (chấm linh hoạt theo logic ngữ pháp)
2. Sửa: "I would like to visit my aunt next week." (would like to + V nguyên thể có "to", không dùng V-ing)
3. Gợi ý: He should study harder every day. (chấm theo logic, miễn đúng cấu trúc "should + V")
4. We are going camping next week.
