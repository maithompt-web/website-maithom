Tuần 1 — Unit 11: What's the matter with you? (Phần 1)

Bám sát SGK Tiếng Anh 5 - Global Success (Học kỳ 2)

📘 A. Lý thuyết

1. Hỏi thăm sức khỏe: "What's the matter with you?" (Bạn bị làm sao vậy? / Bạn cảm thấy thế nào?)

2. Trả lời về bệnh: "I have a + tên bệnh." (Tôi bị...)

— Với các bệnh bắt đầu bằng phụ âm, ta dùng a: a headache, a stomachache, a toothache, a fever, a cough, a cold.

Ví dụ: What's the matter with you? – I have a headache. I feel very tired.

🎨 B. Từ vựng minh họa

HìnhTừ vựngPhiên âm (IPA)Nghĩa
🤕headache/ˈhedeɪk/đau đầu
🤢stomachache/ˈstʌməkeɪk/đau bụng
🦷toothache/ˈtuːθeɪk/đau răng
🌡️fever/ˈfiːvər/sốt
😷cough/kɒf/ho
🤧cold/kəʊld/cảm lạnh

✏️ C. Luyện tập Cơ bản

Example: What's the matter with you? — I have a headache. (a headache / a cold)

  1. What's the matter with you? — I have a ______. (đau bụng)
  2. He has a ______. He cannot eat because his tooth hurts. (đau răng)
  3. She has a ______. Her body is very hot. (sốt)
  4. I have a ______. I keep saying "cough, cough" all day. (ho)
  5. My brother has a ______. He is sneezing a lot. (cảm lạnh)
  6. What's the ______ with you? (chuyện gì, vấn đề gì)
  7. I have a headache and a ______. My tummy hurts too. (đau bụng)
  8. Write the full answer: (What's the matter with you? — headache)

🚀 D. Luyện tập Nâng cao

Example: Write the question for this answer: I have a fever. → What's the matter with you?

  1. Write the question: I have a toothache. →
  2. Find and correct the mistake: "I have headache."
  3. Complete the sentence: My sister ______ (have) a stomachache today.
  4. Put the words in order: matter / the / what's / you / with / ?
  5. Choose the correct word: I have a ______ (fever / doctor).
  6. Fill in: He is coughing a lot. He has a ______. (cough)
  7. Translate into English: Em bị đau đầu và sốt.
  8. Write about a sickness you had before, using "I had a...":

📖 E. Đọc hiểu

Yesterday, Nam felt very tired at school. He had a headache and a fever. His face was red and hot. His teacher, Miss Lan, saw that Nam looked sick. She asked, "What's the matter with you, Nam?" Nam said, "I have a headache and a fever." Miss Lan called Nam's mother. Nam's mother came to school and took him home. He rested and felt better the next day.
  1. How did Nam feel at school? A. happy   B. tired and sick   C. hungry
  2. What was the matter with Nam? A. a toothache   B. a headache and a fever   C. a cough
  3. True or False: Nam's face was red and hot.
  4. Who took Nam home? A. his teacher   B. his friend   C. his mother
  5. True or False: Nam felt better the next day.

✍️ F. Viết đoạn ngắn

Hãy viết 3-4 câu kể về lần gần nhất em bị ốm. Gợi ý: Em bị bệnh gì (headache/stomachache/fever/cough/cold)? Em cảm thấy thế nào lúc đó? Ai đã chăm sóc em? Em có đi học không?

Tuần 2 — Unit 11: What's the matter with you? (Phần 2)

Bám sát SGK Tiếng Anh 5 - Global Success (Học kỳ 2)

📘 A. Lý thuyết

1. Đưa lời khuyên: Dùng động từ khiếm khuyết should để đưa lời khuyên.

— Khẳng định: You should + động từ nguyên mẫu. Ví dụ: You should see a doctor.

— Phủ định: You shouldn't + động từ nguyên mẫu. Ví dụ: You shouldn't go to school today.

Ví dụ: I have a cold. – You should drink water and stay in bed. Get well soon!

🎨 B. Từ vựng minh họa

HìnhTừ vựngPhiên âm (IPA)Nghĩa
👩‍⚕️see a doctor/siː ə ˈdɒktər/đi khám bác sĩ
💊take medicine/teɪk ˈmedsn/uống thuốc
🛌rest/rest/nghỉ ngơi
💧drink water/drɪŋk ˈwɔːtər/uống nước
🛏️stay in bed/steɪ ɪn bed/nằm nghỉ trên giường
💐get well soon/ɡet wel suːn/mau khỏe nhé

✏️ C. Luyện tập Cơ bản

Example: I have a headache. — You should rest. (rest / run)

  1. I have a fever. — You should ______. (uống nước)
  2. He has a toothache. — He should ______. (đi khám bác sĩ)
  3. She has a bad cold. — She should ______. (nằm nghỉ trên giường)
  4. I have a stomachache. — You should ______. (uống thuốc)
  5. He should ______ before he plays football again. (nghỉ ngơi)
  6. You ______ see a doctor if you have a high fever. (should / shouldn't)
  7. My friend is sick. I say to her: "______!" (mau khỏe nhé)
  8. Write the full answer: (I have a cough. — advice: drink water)

🚀 D. Luyện tập Nâng cao

Example: Write advice for this problem: He has a fever. → He should see a doctor.

  1. Write advice: She has a toothache. →
  2. Find and correct the mistake: "You should to rest."
  3. Complete the sentence: You ______ (not/go) to school today. You are very sick.
  4. Put the words in order: should / you / a / doctor / see / .
  5. Choose should/shouldn't: You ______ eat cold ice cream when you have a cough.
  6. Fill in: Drink a lot of ______ when you have a fever. (water)
  7. Translate into English: Bạn nên nghỉ ngơi và uống nhiều nước.
  8. Write one piece of advice you would give a sick friend, using "You should...":

📖 E. Đọc hiểu

Last week, Mai had a bad cold. She had a cough and felt very tired. Her mother said, "What's the matter with you?" Mai said, "I have a cold and a cough." Her mother said, "You should rest and drink a lot of water. You should also take some medicine. You shouldn't go outside in the cold wind." Mai stayed in bed for two days. She felt much better and said, "Thank you, Mom!"
  1. What was the matter with Mai? A. a fever   B. a cold and a cough   C. a headache
  2. What did Mai's mother tell her to do? A. play outside   B. rest and drink water   C. go to school
  3. True or False: Mai's mother told her to go outside in the cold wind.
  4. How long did Mai stay in bed? A. one day   B. two days   C. one week
  5. True or False: Mai felt much better after resting.

✍️ F. Viết đoạn ngắn

Hãy viết 3-4 câu đưa lời khuyên cho một người bạn đang bị cảm. Gợi ý: Bạn ấy bị làm sao? Em khuyên bạn nên làm gì (should)? Em khuyên bạn không nên làm gì (shouldn't)? Em chúc bạn điều gì?

Tuần 3 — Unit 12: What does your father do? (Phần 1)

Bám sát SGK Tiếng Anh 5 - Global Success (Học kỳ 2)

📘 A. Lý thuyết

1. Hỏi nghề nghiệp: "What does your father do?" (Bố bạn làm nghề gì?) / "What does your mother do?" (Mẹ bạn làm nghề gì?)

2. Trả lời: "He is a/an + nghề nghiệp." (nếu là nam) — "She is a/an + nghề nghiệp." (nếu là nữ)

— Dùng an trước nghề bắt đầu bằng nguyên âm: an engineer. Dùng a trước các nghề còn lại.

Ví dụ: What does your father do? – He is a doctor. What does your mother do? – She is a nurse.

🎨 B. Từ vựng minh họa

HìnhTừ vựngPhiên âm (IPA)Nghĩa
👨‍⚕️doctor/ˈdɒktər/bác sĩ
👩‍🏫teacher/ˈtiːtʃər/giáo viên
👷engineer/ˌendʒɪˈnɪər/kỹ sư
🌾farmer/ˈfɑːmər/nông dân
🚗driver/ˈdraɪvər/tài xế
👩‍⚕️nurse/nɜːs/y tá

✏️ C. Luyện tập Cơ bản

Example: What does your father do? — He is a doctor. (a doctor / a farmer)

  1. What does your mother do? — She is a ______. (giáo viên)
  2. My father is an ______. He builds bridges and roads. (kỹ sư)
  3. My grandfather is a ______. He grows rice in the field. (nông dân)
  4. My uncle is a ______. He drives a bus every day. (tài xế)
  5. My aunt is a ______. She helps doctors at the hospital. (y tá)
  6. What ______ your father do? (does / do)
  7. He ______ (be) a doctor. He works at a big hospital.
  8. Write the full answer: (What does your father do? — engineer)

🚀 D. Luyện tập Nâng cao

Example: Write the question for this answer: My mother is a teacher. → What does your mother do?

  1. Write the question: My father is a farmer. →
  2. Find and correct the mistake: "My father is engineer."
  3. Complete the sentence: My mother ______ (work) at a school. She is a teacher.
  4. Put the words in order: father / what / your / does / do / ?
  5. Choose a/an: She is ______ nurse. (a / an)
  6. Fill in: He fixes cars and drives a taxi. He is a ______. (driver)
  7. Translate into English: Bố tôi là bác sĩ. Mẹ tôi là giáo viên.
  8. Write about your father's or mother's job, using "He/She is a...":

📖 E. Đọc hiểu

Hello, my name is Phong. I want to tell you about my parents' jobs. My father is a doctor. He works at a hospital in the city. He helps sick people every day. My mother is a teacher. She teaches Math at a primary school. She loves her students very much. My parents work hard every day. I am very proud of them.
  1. What does Phong's father do? A. a teacher   B. a doctor   C. a farmer
  2. Where does Phong's father work? A. at a school   B. at a hospital   C. at a farm
  3. True or False: Phong's mother is a nurse.
  4. What subject does Phong's mother teach? A. English   B. Math   C. Music
  5. True or False: Phong is proud of his parents.

✍️ F. Viết đoạn ngắn

Hãy viết 3-4 câu giới thiệu nghề nghiệp của bố hoặc mẹ em. Gợi ý: Bố/mẹ em làm nghề gì? Bố/mẹ em làm việc ở đâu? Công việc đó như thế nào? Em cảm thấy thế nào về công việc của bố/mẹ?

Tuần 4 — Unit 12: What does your father do? (Phần 2)

Bám sát SGK Tiếng Anh 5 - Global Success (Học kỳ 2)

A. Lý thuyết

1. Hỏi về nơi làm việc: Dùng "Where" kết hợp với trợ động từ "does/do" để hỏi một người làm việc ở đâu.

Cấu trúc: Where + does/do + S + work? — S + works/work + at + nơi làm việc.

Ví dụ: Where does he work? He works at a hospital.

2. Một số nơi làm việc thường gặp: hospital (bệnh viện), school (trường học), factory (nhà máy), farm (nông trại), office (văn phòng), company (công ty).

Ví dụ: Where do you work? I work at a school. Where does your father work? He works at a company.

B. Từ vựng minh họa

Hình ảnhTừ vựngPhiên âm (IPA)Nghĩa tiếng Việt
🏥hospital/ˈhɒspɪtl/bệnh viện
🏫school/skuːl/trường học
🏭factory/ˈfæktri/nhà máy
🚜farm/fɑːm/nông trại
🏢office/ˈɒfɪs/văn phòng
🏬company/ˈkʌmpəni/công ty

C. Luyện tập Cơ bản

Example: My father is a doctor. He works at a hospital (hospital).
  1. My mother is a teacher. She works ___________ (school).
  2. My uncle is a worker. He works ___________ (factory).
  3. My grandfather is a farmer. He works ___________ (farm).
  4. My sister is a secretary. She works ___________ (office).
  5. My brother is a businessman. He works ___________ (company).
  6. Where ___________ your father work? (does/do)
  7. She ___________ (work) at a hospital every day.
  8. They ___________ (work) at a factory near their house.

D. Luyện tập Nâng cao

Example: works / He / a hospital / at → He works at a hospital.
  1. school / She / at / works
  2. does / Where / work / your mother?
  3. factory / at / a / He works
  4. farm / a / at / works / My grandfather
  5. an office / at / works / My sister
  6. a company / at / works / My father
  7. do / you / Where / work?
  8. hospital / a / at / works / The doctor

E. Đọc hiểu

Hi, I'm Nam. Let me tell you about my family's jobs. My father is a doctor. He works at a hospital in the city. My mother is a teacher. She works at a primary school near my house. My uncle is a factory worker. He works at a factory every day. My grandfather is a farmer. He works at a farm in the countryside. I love my family very much.

  1. What does Nam's father do?
  2. Where does he work?
  3. What is Nam's mother's job?
  4. Where does she work?
  5. What does Nam's grandfather do?

F. Viết đoạn ngắn

Viết 3-4 câu về nghề nghiệp và nơi làm việc của một người thân trong gia đình em. Gợi ý: Who is he/she? What does he/she do? Where does he/she work?

Tuần 5 — Unit 13: What happened in the story?

Bám sát SGK Tiếng Anh 5 - Global Success (Học kỳ 2)

A. Lý thuyết

1. Kể chuyện bằng thì quá khứ đơn: Dùng thì quá khứ đơn (Past Simple) để kể lại những sự việc đã xảy ra trong một câu chuyện.

Cấu trúc: S + V(ed/quá khứ bất quy tắc) + O.

Ví dụ: What happened in the story? A boy found a treasure.

2. Một số động từ bất quy tắc thường gặp trong truyện kể: find → found, go → went, see → saw, have → had, begin → began.

Ví dụ: The story began with a little girl. She saw a magic door.

B. Từ vựng minh họa

Hình ảnhTừ vựngPhiên âm (IPA)Nghĩa tiếng Việt
📖story/ˈstɔːri/câu chuyện
💥happen/ˈhæpən/xảy ra
🔤begin/bɪˈɡɪn/bắt đầu
🏁end/end/kết thúc
🧑‍🎤character/ˈkærəktər/nhân vật
🗺️adventure/ədˈventʃər/cuộc phiêu lưu

C. Luyện tập Cơ bản

Example: A boy found (find) a treasure in the forest.
  1. The story ___________ (begin) a long time ago.
  2. A boy ___________ (find) a treasure box.
  3. He ___________ (open) the box carefully.
  4. The character ___________ (see) a dragon.
  5. They ___________ (have) a great adventure.
  6. The story ___________ (end) happily.
  7. What ___________ (happen) next in the story?
  8. The girl ___________ (go) into a magic forest.

D. Luyện tập Nâng cao

Example: began / story / The / a long time ago → The story began a long time ago.
  1. found / A boy / a treasure
  2. happened / What / in the story?
  3. saw / The girl / a dragon
  4. adventure / had / They / an exciting
  5. ended / happily / The story
  6. character / brave / The / was
  7. went / boy / into / The / the forest
  8. begin / did / the story / When?

E. Đọc hiểu

Once upon a time, there was a small village near a forest. One day, a boy named Tom went into the forest to find his lost dog. Suddenly, he saw a strange cave. He walked inside and found an old treasure box. Inside the box, there was a golden key. Tom took the key home and showed it to his family. It was the beginning of a great adventure.

  1. Where did Tom go?
  2. What did he see in the forest?
  3. What did he find in the cave?
  4. What was inside the box?
  5. What did Tom do with the key?

F. Viết đoạn ngắn

Viết 3-4 câu kể tiếp câu chuyện về Tom và chiếc chìa khóa vàng, hoặc kể về một câu chuyện em thích. Gợi ý: What happened next? Who were the characters? How did the story end?

Tuần 6 — Ôn tập & Kiểm tra 15 phút (Unit 11-13)

Bám sát SGK Tiếng Anh 5 - Global Success (Học kỳ 2)

Nội dung ôn tập: Unit 11 (bệnh vặt và lời khuyên), Unit 12 (nghề nghiệp và nơi làm việc), Unit 13 (kể chuyện bằng thì quá khứ đơn).

Phần I. Trắc nghiệm (8 câu x 0.75 điểm = 6 điểm)

Khoanh tròn vào chữ cái A, B, C hoặc D trước câu trả lời đúng.

  1. What's the matter with you? - I have a ___.
    A. headache   B. hospital   C. doctor   D. teacher
  2. You have a toothache. You should see a ___.
    A. teacher   B. dentist   C. farmer   D. driver
  3. What does your father do? - He ___ a doctor.
    A. is   B. does   C. has   D. works
  4. Where ___ your mother work? - She works at a school.
    A. do   B. does   C. is   D. are
  5. My uncle works ___ a factory.
    A. in   B. at   C. on   D. to
  6. The story ___ with a little girl in a forest.
    A. begin   B. begins   C. began   D. beginning
  7. A boy ___ a treasure in the cave yesterday.
    A. find   B. finds   C. found   D. finding
  8. What ___ in the story? - A dragon appeared.
    A. happen   B. happens   C. happened   D. happening

Phần II. Viết lại câu / Điền từ (4 câu x 1 điểm = 4 điểm)

  1. Sắp xếp từ thành câu hoàn chỉnh: a headache / have / I.
  2. Điền từ thích hợp (should): You have a fever. You ___ see a doctor.
  3. Viết lại câu sang thì quá khứ đơn: They go to the forest and find a treasure. (Yesterday, ...)
  4. Điền từ để hỏi đúng: "___ does your father work?" - "He works at a company."
